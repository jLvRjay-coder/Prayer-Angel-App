# The Prophets: God's Call, Mercy, and Hope

Season 3 of Daily Encouragement. 126 daily devotionals, June 3 through October 6, 2027, following The Stories Jesus Told.

The series moves through the Major Prophets and then all twelve Minor Prophets in English Bible order. Historical transitions explain the divided kingdom, exile, return, and differing settings. This is a selected-passage journey through the full storyline, rather than an exhaustive commentary on every verse.

Every day includes a full selected KJV reading and complete key verse, original context and explanation, a connection across Scripture, a prayer, and an open personal question. Existing publication dates and devotional content are preserved.
