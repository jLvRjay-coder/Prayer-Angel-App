Beyond The Message

Last Updated: March 2025

Beyond the Message (“we,” “our,” or “the App”) provides spiritual study tools, devotional content, and AI-assisted reflections rooted in Scripture. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

Information We Collect

1. Information You Provide

  • Questions you submit to the Guardian Angel feature
  • Reflections you write in the Daily Compass
  • Optional study notes entered into text boxes

We do not require registration, accounts, or personal identity information.

2. Automatically Collected Information

We may collect standard analytics data, such as:

  • Device type
  • Browser type
  • Usage statistics
  • General location (city / country)

This helps us improve app performance.

3. AI Processing

Some content in the App (including Guardian Angel responses) is generated using OpenAI’s API.

  • Your text input is securely sent to OpenAI for processing.
  • OpenAI does not use your data to train public models.
  • We do not store personal identifiers alongside your content.

How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver personalized spiritual reflections
  • To improve app quality, stability, and experience
  • To detect and fix technical issues

We do not sell or share your information with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Children’s Privacy

Beyond the Message is not directed to children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Data Security

We use HTTPS encryption and industry-standard safeguards.
However, no online service is 100% secure, and you use the App at your own risk.

Not Medical or Crisis Advice

The App offers Scripture-based spiritual guidance.
It is not a substitute for:

  • Licensed therapy
  • Medical treatment
  • Emergency services

If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page.
